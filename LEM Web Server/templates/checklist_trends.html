The QC wall can colour because a QC standard states its own band; this cannot, and the difference is the point. WHAT IT CAN SAY. Movement, and recency. The sparkline shows the shape, the number shows where it is now, the delta shows which way it went, and an item nobody has written down leads the page — that is the only finding a checklist dashboard can honestly make, and it is exactly what a round exists to catch. #}
