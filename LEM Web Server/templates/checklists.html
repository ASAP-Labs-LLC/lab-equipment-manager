Edit checklist

An item can just be ticked, or it can record a reading — number for anything you want to track over time (a cylinder at 2900 PSI), text for logging anything ("half full").

Editing changes the checklist itself, for every day — not just today. Days already recorded show the new wording too, and removing an item removes it from those days' totals. Ticks and readings already logged are kept.

Drag the handle to reorder, or use the arrows. Each item's settings sit under its text.
